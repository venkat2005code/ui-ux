Phase 04

Structural Wireframing

Before applying visual aesthetics, we focus entirely on functionality and layout. Low-fidelity wireframes act as blueprints to validate the core user flows rapidly.

"Form follows function. Wireframes allow us to iterate on structure without the distraction of colors and fonts."
